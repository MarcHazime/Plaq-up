# Glossary

**Plate**: a French SIV license plate in the form `AA-123-BB`. Identifies a Vehicle.

**Vehicle**: a car registered by a User under one Plate. A User has one primary Vehicle and any number of secondary ones.

**User**: a person with an account who owns Vehicles and sends Messages.

**Message**: a text or image sent from one User to another. It may carry a related Plate as context. Each side can delete it for themselves only.

**Conversation**: all Messages exchanged between one pair of Users, whatever Plate they relate to.

**Block**: one User preventing another from sending them Messages.
